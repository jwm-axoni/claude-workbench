# Dream log

What the `nightly-dream` routine read, learned and changed. The dream owns this file.
{{preferred_name}} reads it, and the morning briefing surfaces the latest entry under
"From the dream". Newest entry at the top of `## Entries`.

## How to read an entry

- **Mode**: `report-only` (wrote nothing outside this file) or `live`
- **Read**: the recap, captures and meeting files it worked from
- **Updated**: pages it changed, dream-owned sections only, each with what it traces to
- **New**: people, projects, orgs, tools or terms it found with no page yet
- **Needs your yes**: things it wasn't sure enough to write. Tick `- [x]` to approve, delete the line to decline.
- **Learned about how {{preferred_name}} works**: repeated friction, repeated manual work, ideas worth a playbook or skill
- **Lookups**: any source call it made because the vault didn't answer

## Confidence bar

Starts at: status 0.70 · commitment 0.80 · decision/person/term 0.90. The weekly moves these
on evidence from `Tracking/Signals/`, never on a hunch.
