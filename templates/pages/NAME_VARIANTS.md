# Name variants

Transcripts, dictation and quick notes mangle names. Check here before using a name from one.
Rendering a variant as a separate person is the most common way a briefing goes wrong.

| Heard as | Is | State | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| _example: Carrie_ | _[[Kerry Smith]]_ | _confirmed_ | _2026-01-01 recap_ |

`State` is `confirmed` ({{preferred_name}} or a source said so) or `UNCONFIRMED` (a working
guess, report it as unresolved). `nightly-dream` adds rows; {{preferred_name}} confirms them.
